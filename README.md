**The 5 Minute Life – Excel Project**



**Project Overview**



The 5 Minute Life Excel Project is a data analysis and visualization project built using Microsoft Excel. It tracks and analyzes daily journeys, delays, costs, and commuting behavior to provide insights into time management, efficiency, and travel optimization.





**Dataset Contents**



The dataset captures structured journey records with attributes such as:



Record ID – Unique identifier for each trip



Date \& Day – Travel occurrence



Journey Purpose – College, Work, Shopping, Social, Appointment, Errand



Planned vs Actual Arrival – Scheduled vs real arrival time



Delay Minutes – Difference between planned and actual arrival



Distance (km) – Travel distance



Transport Mode – Bus, Train, Auto, Cab, Two‑wheeler, Walk



Planned \& Extra Cost (₹) – Expected fare and additional expenses



Missed Connection – Indicator of failed connecting transport



Waiting Time (min) – Time spent waiting



Total Time Lost (min) – Delay + waiting combined



Delay Reason – Traffic, Weather, Late Departure, Personal Delay, Waiting



Weather – Clear, Cloudy, Rain, Hot



Peak Status – Peak vs Non‑Peak travel hours



Delay Category – On Time, Minor, Moderate, Major





&#x20;**Objectives**



Identify patterns of delays across transport modes



Analyze common causes of time loss (traffic, weather, waiting)



Compare planned vs actual costs and highlight extra expenses



Study peak vs non‑peak travel efficiency



Provide commuting behavior insights by journey purpose





**Features**



Data Cleaning \& Formatting – Structured dataset for analysis



Pivot Tables – Summarized insights by transport mode and delay reason



Charts \& Graphs – Visual representation of delays, costs, and time lost



Categorization – Delay classified into On Time, Minor, Moderate, Major



Interactive Dashboard – Quick insights into commuting efficiency





**Tools \& Skills Used**



Microsoft Excel (Advanced) – Pivot tables, charts, conditional formatting, dashboards



Data Analysis – Delay patterns, cost analysis, time management insights



Visualization – Graphs and dashboards for clear presentation



Problem Solving – Identifying inefficiencies and optimizing travel data
